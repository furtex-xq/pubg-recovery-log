Case file · PUBG Mobile

Account Recovery Log

A dated record of every attempt to get one PUBG Mobile account back: what was sent, what support answered, and the reason given each time.

Background

The account is about three years old, played since season C4S12, and reached Conqueror in September 2025. Around 6 March 2026 the owner lent the login to a friend. Within a day the linked sign-in methods were changed and the owner was locked out. The loss was only discovered a few months later, on returning to the game.

All claims below were made by the original owner through official channels only: the in-game Account Recovery tool and customer service email.
